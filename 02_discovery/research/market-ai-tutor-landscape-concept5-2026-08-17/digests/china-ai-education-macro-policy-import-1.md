## Claims resting on a single named publisher only

Nearly every claim row above rests on exactly **one** named publisher/document — this report does not, anywhere, corroborate a specific market-size, growth, or funding figure with two independently-authored sources for the *same* number. Where two dated releases are cited for a running series (e.g., CAC's Jan 2026 and May 2026 GenAI filing statistics; MOE's Apr 2025 and Apr 2026 policy documents), these are two releases from the **same** publisher/agency, not independent corroboration.

Single-source rows, by topic:
- **Market-size/growth (all rows):** iResearch alone (GenAI+ education market total, decomposition, school/consumer split, 2028 forecast, higher-ed/K-12 procurement share); Grand View Research alone (US$721.9m 2025 figure and 2026–2033 CAGR); RUNTO alone (AI learning-tablet units/value); iiMedia Research alone (smart-learning-machine and smart-hardware figures). No two of these are corroborating the same metric — this is precisely why the report treats the iResearch vs. Grand View Research figures as a genuine, unresolved contradiction rather than a verified consensus number, and why it states in its own Research Quality Note that market sizing is only "Medium" confidence and pure B2C AI-tutor TAM / AI-EdTech investment totals are "Medium-low."
- **Regulatory rows (all rows):** each regulation is sourced to a single issuing authority's own publication (CAC, Ministry of Education, Ministry of Public Security, or joint issuances treated as one source) — there is no independent secondary confirmation of any regulation's text or effective date.
