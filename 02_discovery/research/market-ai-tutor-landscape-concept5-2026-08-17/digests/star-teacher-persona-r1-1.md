# Digest: AI "star-teacher" persona archetype — round 1

Brief: Market precedent, legal risk, sentiment, and pricing for AI-tutor products that personify/clone a specific renowned "star" teacher's voice/likeness/style, in K-12/children/ESL EdTech (China, US, India, SEA, Vietnam), to inform whether this is a viable mass-market ($10-20/mo) product archetype.
Stopped because: novelty exhaustion — after 8 WebSearch queries + 5 WebFetch attempts (3 succeeded, 2 blocked/404/429) across 18 tool calls, the last search round (pricing, India-adjacent, legal corroboration) returned no new named competitor or load-bearing claim not already surfaced. Budget guidance (~15 calls) was modestly exceeded to chase corroboration on the highest-stakes legal claim; stopping here rather than opening a third search thread.

### [14] Lovo Inc. voice-actor lawsuit — right-of-publicity claims survive motion to dismiss under NY "digital replica" law

## Disputed / contradictory findings

- **General AI-tutor sentiment tension**: [19] Superprof-cited survey suggests strong parent/tutor skepticism that AI will ever match human tutoring quality (78% of parents said AI would "never" overtake human tutors), while [20] the (older, 2023, stale) Intelligent.com survey found the large majority of students/parents who had tried both preferred ChatGPT over in-person tutoring for grade improvement. These are not strictly contradictory — one asks about long-run belief in AI "overtaking" humans, the other about immediate practical preference for a study aid — but they point in different directions and neither was independently verified via primary source this session. Do not treat either as a settled fact without re-verification.
- No other direct claim-vs-claim conflicts were found; most gaps are absence-of-evidence rather than disagreement between sources.
