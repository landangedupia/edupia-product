## Leads
- **Circular 29 (Thông tư 29) tutoring-ban controversy** — a broader, currently-live regulatory/parent-sentiment flashpoint around "bundled/linked" school programs in Vietnam, directly adjacent to the iSMART Đắk Lắk finding. Worth a dedicated round-2 search ("Thông tư 29" + trường/app name) since it's exactly the "unwanted bundled content" risk this decision cares about, and is clearly documented rather than speculative.
- Edu2Review.com / 1900.com.vn are structurally the right independent-aggregator sites but yielded thin results this round — a round-2 pass should fetch their pages for VUS, ELSA Speak, Yola, iSMART directly rather than relying on search-engine summaries.
- Individual Vietnamese learners do post granular AI-feature critiques on social platforms (Threads) — a dedicated Threads/Facebook-public-post search (not generic web search) could surface more.
- Tinhte.vn forum threads on Yola / 51Talk-vs-Cambly-Kids exist but returned HTTP 403 on fetch — retry via cached/snippet access.

## Not found
- No verified, directly-quoted Vietnamese parent review explicitly praising or distrusting an "AI diagnoses exactly what your child is weak at" claim for a K-12 English app. Closest is the generic ELSA AI-accuracy hedge (secondary/editorial, low confidence). **This angle is thin — not resolved.**
- No direct evidence (positive or negative) of Vietnamese parents specifically valuing or dismissing a "homeroom teacher"/dedicated-teacher-continuity role vs. rotating teachers. VUS's own blog gestures at continuity value but via an unverifiable brand-quoted line. **Genuine gap, not just weak signal.**
- No actual Google Play / Apple App Store review text (rating + body) was retrieved for any of the 7 platforms — generic web search/WebFetch could not reach live app-store review listings. Every "review" source found was a third-party blog, brand page, or forum/social post. **This is the single biggest coverage gap relative to the brief's suggested primary source.**
- Adjacent-market (China/India/SEA) fallback evidence was not pursued — budget spent establishing a Vietnam-specific baseline first.
- Facebook parenting-group posts not directly retrieved (generic search doesn't surface FB group content well).
