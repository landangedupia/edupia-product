# Digest: Tier definitions + household demographics/income

## Claims

- claim: No official/standardized "Tier 1/2/3/4" city classification system for Vietnam was found published by Nielsen Vietnam, Kantar Worldpanel, Q&Me, or Decision Lab in searchable form. The informal convention used by some market commentary is: Tier 1 = Hà Nội + TP.HCM (sometimes "special metropolis" class); Tier 2 = secondary provincial cities (Vinh, Huế, Quy Nhơn, Nha Trang, Đà Lạt, Buôn Ma Thuột, etc.); Tier 3/4 loosely = smaller towns/rural. This is informal marketing-industry shorthand, not a codified standard with public thresholds.

- claim: Vietnam's only official, codified multi-tier urban classification is "đô thị loại I–V và đô thị loại đặc biệt" under Nghị quyết 1210/2016/UBTVQH13 (as amended), based on population/density/infrastructure of specific urban centers, not on whole provinces. Thresholds: Loại I (centrally-run city) ≥1,000,000 total urban pop / ≥500,000 inner-urban; Loại I (provincial/district-level city) ≥500,000 total / ≥200,000 inner-urban; Loại II ≥200,000 total / ≥100,000 inner-urban; Loại III ≥100,000 total / ≥50,000 inner-urban/inner-market; Loại IV ≥50,000 total / ≥20,000 inner-market (if any); overall urban density ≥3,000 people/km², inner-urban construction-area density ≥12,000 people/km².
  source: https://luatminhkhue.vn/do-thi-loai-i-ii-iii-iv-v-va-do-thi-loai-dac-biet-tai-viet-nam.aspx ; https://thuvienphapluat.vn/chinh-sach-phap-luat-moi/vn/ho-tro-phap-luat/tu-van-phap-luat/60139/tieu-chuan-phan-loai-do-thi-loai-dac-biet-loai-i-ii-iii-iv-va-v-moi-nhat

- claim: IMPORTANT MISMATCH FLAG — the user's proposed definition (tier 3 = huyện/district-level towns; tier 4 = any non-centrally-governed province) does not correspond to any single standard system found. It is closest in spirit to the regional-minimum-wage Vùng I–IV system (see wage-policy claims below), which is assigned at DISTRICT level within every province — meaning a "tier 4" province under the user's definition (e.g. Đồng Nai, Bình Dương) can still contain Vùng I/II (high-wage, urbanized) districts such as Biên Hòa city, while a "tier 3" huyện in the same province can be Vùng III/IV. Treating "non-central province" as uniformly low-tier will conflate materially different income districts.

This is a significant structural change worth its own research thread before finalizing the persona geography, since post-merger provincial counts/boundaries differ from the VHLSS 2022/2023 63-province baseline used in the income data above.
- No source found gives a direct, named count of "households with school-age (6-15) children in tier 3/4 areas" — this figure would need to be derived (GSO age-structure tables x rural/non-5-city population share x average household size), not looked up directly. Flagged as a gap below.
