# Digest: Spending behavior & pain points (tier 3/4 English education)

## Claims

- claim: Vietnam's household "Living Standards Survey" (VHLSS) 2020 found average education spending was 7.0 million VND/student/year nationally (+7% vs. 2018). Urban households spent 10.7 million VND/student/year — 2.1x the rural figure (implying rural ≈ 5.1 million VND/student/year). Highest-income households spent 15.4 million VND/student/year vs. 2.5 million VND for lowest-income households (6.2x gap). Southeast region (incl. HCMC) spent 11.0 million VND/student/year, 3.6x the North Central/Central Highlands region (3.1 million VND) — the lowest-spending region in the country.
  source: https://www.nso.gov.vn/du-lieu-va-so-lieu-thong-ke/2021/09/chi-tieu-cho-giao-duc-dao-tao-cua-cac-ho-gia-dinh-o-viet-nam-nhung-nam-gan-day/
  publisher: General Statistics Office of Vietnam (GSO/Tổng cục Thống kê)
  pub_date: 2021-09
  accessed: 2026-08-18
  confidence: high
  class: spending (household, all-education, not English-specific)

- claim: In the same GSO breakdown, of total household education spending, tuition/exam fees = 35.1% (2.5M VND), "supplementary education" (học thêm, which includes tutoring/extracurricular) = 17.5% (1.2M VND), other education costs = 26.6% (1.9M VND). Figures are national averages, not broken out by urban/rural or by subject (English not isolated).

## Leads for follow-up

- No source this run directly surveyed tier-3 (district-town) or tier-4 (non-central-province) parents specifically on English-spending share; all quantified spending data found is either (a) national/urban-vs-rural at the whole-education level (GSO/VHLSS 2020, now 5+ years old — a newer VHLSS round, likely 2022 or 2024, should exist and would tighten the freshness bar) or (b) major-city-weighted market data (EY-Parthenon). A targeted follow-up should search directly for VHLSS 2022/2024 releases and for any Q&Me, Decision Lab, or Nielsen Vietnam report segmenting by "tỉnh," "nông thôn," or specific tier-3/4 province names (e.g., Thái Bình, Nam Định, Nghệ An, Đắk Lắk).
- The EY-Parthenon report (fetched via summarization tool, not the raw PDF/SlideShare) likely contains a full tier/region price table and parent-survey demographic breakdown that could confirm or refute whether any of its 350 parents were tier-3/4 residents — worth locating and reading the primary deck directly next round.
- Gender/role of household decision-maker (mother vs. father) for English-course purchases: no quantified Vietnam-specific data found this run despite two targeted searches; only qualitative "parents/mothers are closest to the child" content-marketing text surfaced. This remains an open question.
- Direct 1-3★ app-store reviews (Monkey Junior, Elsa Speak, Duolingo Vietnamese-market reviews) were not successfully retrieved as primary text this run — WebSearch returned only secondary summaries/blog citations. A follow-up should fetch Google Play / App Store review pages directly.
- Competing Vietnamese ed-tech products explicitly marketed to tier-3/4 or "khu vực tỉnh" audiences (pricing, positioning) were not identified — VioEdu, Clevai, Marathon Education were found but without tier-3/4-specific pricing or geographic-targeting evidence.
- The Dân Việt marketing-complaints piece and telesales vendor pages are Hanoi/national in scope, not tier-3/4-verified — worth searching specifically for provincial-press (báo địa phương) consumer-complaint coverage of English-center or app sales tactics in specific tier-3/4 provinces.
