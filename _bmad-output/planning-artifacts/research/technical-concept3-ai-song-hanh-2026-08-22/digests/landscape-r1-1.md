# Landscape & Maturity — AI Song Hành technical feasibility (Round 1)

## Findings

- **Khan Academy's Khanmigo (launched 2023) shows persistently low engagement in production: only ~15% of students with access actually use it regularly, prompting a full redesign rolling out summer 2026.** Sal Khan himself called it "a non-event" for most students who "just didn't use it much," and Khan Academy's own leadership (DiCerbo) describes interactions skewing toward passive "IDK IDK" patterns rather than the sustained, relationship-like engagement a persona-based system needs. [source: EdTech Innovation Hub / Chalkbeat (via Dan Meyer digest), Apr 2026, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: medium, class: production/adoption metric — single detailed source (Dan Meyer substack) plus one corroborating search snippet, not independently re-verified against Khan Academy's own primary statement] https://danmeyer.substack.com/p/rip-khanmigo-and-edtech-industry ; corroborating: https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/only-15-percent-of-students-with-access-to-khanmigo-actually-use-it-khan-academy-admits

- **CORRECTION/CONTRADICTION FLAG:** An initial read of the Dan Meyer piece produced the claim "Khanmigo was discontinued by April 2026." A follow-up search directly contradicts this: Khanmigo was NOT shut down — it remains live, scored well in Khan Academy's internal A/B tests, and is being redesigned rather than killed. The "RIP" framing is rhetorical/critical commentary about underwhelming impact, not a literal shutdown. Treat the "discontinued" framing as false; the accurate claim is "low adoption + redesign," not "killed." [source: search synthesis across Chalkbeat, EdTech Innovation Hub, Khan Academy blog, Aug 2026, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: medium — not independently fetched from Khan Academy's own primary blog post]

- **Quizlet's Q-Chat — "the world's first AI tutor built with OpenAI's ChatGPT" — was fully retired/shut down after June 30, 2025**, per Quizlet's own official statement ("After careful evaluation and customer feedback, we've decided to retire the Q-Chat study mode"). This is a genuine, confirmed production casualty of a continuous/conversational AI tutor product at a major edtech brand, alive under two years. [source: Quizlet official blog/statement, retrieved via search Jun 2025 announcement, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: high for the shutdown fact itself (primary company statement), confidence: low for the "per-user inference costs ate the margins" causal explanation (that attribution comes from a secondary blog, AgentConn, not confirmed by Quizlet), class: technology/product failure — meets two-source bar loosely (Quizlet's own statement + AgentConn commentary), but the *cost causation* claim is single-source and should be treated as an unverified hypothesis, not fact] https://quizlet.com/blog/meet-q-chat (product launch), shutdown fact corroborated via search snippets referencing Quizlet's June 2025 retirement announcement

- **Duolingo's "Lily" is a shipped, production persistent-persona product**: an AI character (drawn from an existing in-app character, not a generic assistant) that holds live spoken video conversations, remembers prior calls, corrects grammar in real time, and required deliberate character-design investment (Duolingo's own team describes tuning her "sarcastic, deadpan" personality against the need to still "feel engaged," using Rive for real-time facial/body animation tied to voice). This is evidence that consistent-persona + voice + continuity-across-sessions is achievable in production today, at least for a narrow language-practice use case — not yet evidence for continuous week-long homework supervision. [source: Rive.app company blog (primary — Rive is the animation vendor Duolingo used, describing their own collaboration), 2025-2026, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: medium, class: shipped product capability] https://rive.app/blog/duolingo-s-ai-powered-video-call-brings-lily-to-life ; feature availability limited to major languages (Spanish, French, English, German), gated behind Duolingo Max paid tier.

- **Real-time voice-cloning/TTS latency has crossed into conversationally-viable territory as of 2025-2026**: streaming TTS first-audio-chunk latency of 80–300ms is now reported for production systems (ElevenLabs Flash, Cartesia Sonic), with zero-shot voice cloning latency pushed below 100ms by ElevenLabs and Cartesia in 2025-2026, and one vendor (Gradium) reporting 258ms P50 time-to-first-audio in its own benchmark. Target for natural conversational feel is sub-500ms end-to-end (human turn-taking gaps average ~200ms). [source: vendor/aggregator blog content (Inworld.ai, Gradium, Forasoft), 2026, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: low — these are vendor-published or vendor-aggregated benchmark numbers, not independently audited; classic red flag (marketing register, numbers sourced from the vendors selling the product); needs independent corroboration before being treated as established] https://inworld.ai/resources/best-voice-ai-tts-apis-for-real-time-voice-agents-2026-benchmarks ; https://gradium.ai/content/best-text-to-speech-api-voice-agents

- **Academic research on "process-aware AI tutors" that continuously monitor student work and decide when to intervene is active and very recent (May 2026) but is lab/design-stage research, not a deployed system.** The paper "Help Me, But Don't Track Me: Intervention Timing and Privacy Boundaries for Process-Aware AI Tutors" (arXiv:2604.06178v2, published 2026-05-05) frames the central unsolved problem explicitly as tension between proactive help and surveillance/privacy — i.e., the exact mechanism "AI Song Hành" needs (continuous monitoring + proactive nudges) is still being actively worked out as a research design problem, not a solved engineering pattern. [source: arXiv preprint, 2026-05-05, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: medium (read via PDF fetch, primary source, but summarization by fetch tool not deeply verified line-by-line), class: research/pattern maturity] https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.06178

- **A second, closely related 2026 paper — "DeepTutor: Towards Agentic Personalized Tutoring" (arXiv:2604.26962) — exists and is framed around agentic (multi-step, autonomous) personalized tutoring**, reinforcing that "agentic tutor that acts over time" is a live 2026 research topic, not yet a settled production pattern. Not fetched in full this round (budget) — title/framing only confirmed via search result metadata. [source: arXiv search listing, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: low (not fetched/read in full), class: research existence only] https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.26962

- **General insight from proactive-intervention research: students prefer low-intensity nudges that respect their own workflow, and researchers suggest a graduated-escalation model where AI intervenes more only when the learner's own self-regulation appears insufficient** — i.e., the emerging design consensus (still research-stage) is to under-intervene by default rather than aggressively push notifications, which is directly relevant to the "proactive nudges" component of AI Song Hành. [source: search-synthesized from academic literature (exact paper not individually fetched), accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: low — this claim was produced by the search engine's synthesis across results, not read from a single identified primary paper; treat as a lead to verify, not an established finding, class: pattern claim]

- **Human-in-the-loop (HITL) is broadly recognized in 2026 general agentic-AI architecture literature as a standard, named design pattern** (approval gates, pause-for-human-review at critical junctures) — consistent with the "teacher reviews AI-collected data before each live session" design in AI Song Hành — but this consensus comes from generic SEO/listicle-style AI-agent content (Augment Code, Sitepoint, Codebridge, Internative, Heym), not education-specific or technically authoritative sources. It should be read as confirming HITL is a *known, named* pattern in the broader agent-engineering discourse, not as evidence that HITL-gated continuous tutoring specifically has a proven implementation. [source: multiple SEO/vendor blogs, 2026, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: low, class: landscape/pattern consolidation claim — genuinely multi-publisher but all secondary/marketing-register content, not primary]

- **Persistent AI "companion" persona products (Character.ai, and smaller entrants SoulLink, KAi, MyPresio) all converge on the same architectural problem in 2026: standard transformer context windows cannot hold long-term relationship history, so every serious platform layers retrieval/summarization/metadata on top of the base model rather than relying on raw context.** This is presented as a widely-acknowledged limitation across multiple companion-AI vendors, not a single vendor's claim, which raises its confidence somewhat despite being drawn from vendor/aggregator content. [source: multiple companion-AI vendor/aggregator blogs, 2026, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: low-medium — consistent across several independent vendors describing their own different solutions to the same problem (some corroborating signal), but all are vendor self-description rather than independent audits, class: landscape/architecture pattern] https://flowith.io/blog/character-ai-building-persistent-companions-remember-you/ ; https://digitalhumancorp.com/en/research/best-ai-companion-apps-with-memory-2026

- **Kristen DiCerbo** (Khan Academy, quoted 2026 re: Khanmigo interaction quality — "IDK IDK" passive-interaction problem) — worth chasing her original statement/interview directly; would be a strong primary source on real production friction with a persona-tutor at scale.

- **Hamilton County Schools (TN) two-year Khanmigo RCT, 2024-25 and 2025-26 school years** — reported math achievement gain of 1.26 national percentile ranks/term. This is a named, dated efficacy study worth pulling directly (likely via GrowthBook's Khan Academy case study or an academic writeup) — it's the most concrete efficacy number found this round and deserves independent verification, ideally from the study itself rather than a blog's summary.
- **DeepTutor (arXiv:2604.26962)** — not read in full; likely directly relevant to "continuous agentic tutoring" state of the art and should be prioritized in a follow-up round.
- **Quizlet Q-Chat's shutdown cause** — the "inference cost economics killed it" narrative is currently attributed to a single secondary source (AgentConn); Quizlet's own public statement gives no cost rationale. Worth searching for founder/exec commentary (e.g., on X/LinkedIn, earnings calls) that might confirm or deny cost-driven shutdown, since a real per-user-inference-cost failure mode is highly material to AI Song Hành's build-vs-risk case.
- **Squirrel AI** (China) came up as an adaptive-learning comparator but was not researched in depth — flagged in one source as unattractive for buyers needing data residency outside China; worth a dedicated round if adaptive-learning engines (vs. persona/voice) become relevant to the decision.
- **ELSA Speak** (Vietnam-founded, English-learning, voice-AI) is the most market-relevant comparator found (same geography, same "self-study English" domain) but this round only surfaced company-background/PR content, not technical or production-maturity detail on its "AI Tutor" (launched 2023) or continuous-monitoring capabilities. Strongly recommend a dedicated follow-up round specifically on ELSA's AI Tutor architecture, usage data, and any public statements about persona consistency or proactive nudging — it is the closest real-world analog to AI Song Hành's market and use case.
- **Contradiction to track**: "Khanmigo discontinued" (from initial single-source read) vs. "Khanmigo redesigned, not discontinued" (from broader search) — resolved in favor of the latter in this digest, but flagged so downstream synthesis doesn't inherit the wrong fact.
