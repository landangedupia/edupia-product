# Architecture patterns in practice — R2 digest

Scope (round 2, following up on R1 gaps): (1) named systems implementing "AI acts continuously, human reviews a periodic BATCH before a scheduled checkpoint" vs. exception-escalation; (2) a primary source for Squirrel AI's "Large Adaptive Model" architecture; (3) TAL/好未来 and Yuanfudao/猿辅导 AI-teacher technical implementation; (4) Spheria.ai mechanism and pricing. Research firewall: findings below are limited to what was retrieved this run.

## Findings

- **The "periodic batch review before a checkpoint" pattern IS a named, described HITL design option in general AI-agent literature — but no named product or documented production deployment implementing it was found, in EdTech or otherwise.** MindStudio's agent-design blog explicitly describes two relevant variants: "Not every checkpoint needs to be synchronous. For non-urgent workflows, batch up flagged items and let a reviewer process them in a single daily pass," and separately, "Periodic audits — No real-time interruption. The agent runs autonomously, but a human reviews a sample of outputs on a regular schedule." This is the closest match found across both research rounds to the AI Song Hành shape (continuous AI action, scheduled human batch review before a cadence point). [source: MindStudio, "Human-in-the-Loop Checkpoints for AI Agents: Why Full Autonomy Is the Wrong Goal", accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: medium, class: architecture pattern] https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/human-in-the-loop-checkpoints-ai-agents-full-autonomy
  - Caveat: this is prescriptive/design-advocacy content from a vendor blog, not a case study of a real deployed system running this pattern. It names the *pattern* and gives MindStudio as the *platform* that could support it (via generic "approval steps"/"conditional routing" primitives), but does not cite any specific customer or product that has actually built the weekly-batch-before-checkpoint shape end to end.
  - A second, independent source (a Medium/Spring AI write-up found via search but not fetched this round) similarly describes a generic "checkpoint-based pause & resume pattern" using persistent state (e.g., LangGraph interrupts), consistent with the same design vocabulary — treat as corroboration that the pattern is discussed in engineering circles, not as a second deployment example.

- **Squirrel AI's own press material (PR Newswire, the company's primary announcement channel) does NOT substantiate the "24 million students / fine-tuned" architecture claim — it was fetched directly this round and contains no model-type, training-data, or fine-tuning specifics at all.** The release describes only capabilities/outcomes (">90% accuracy" identifying student errors across "comprehension, logic, calculation, and handwritten transcription," multimodal text+voice input, "100+ interactive dialogue scenarios," emotion recognition, doubled chapter-assessment performance in an internal trial) with zero technical architecture disclosure — no mention of model type (fine-tuned LLM vs. knowledge graph vs. RAG), no restatement of the "24M students / 10B behavior data points" figure that appeared in earlier (non-Squirrel, aggregated) search snippets. [source: PR Newswire (Squirrel AI's own distributed release), "Squirrel Ai Debuts Enhanced Large Multimodal Adaptive Model...", accessed 2026-08-22 (fetched directly), confidence: high that the disclosure gap is real, class: primary source with negative finding] https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/squirrel-ai-debuts-enhanced-large-multimodal-adaptive-model-revolutionizing-its-educational-software-and-hardware-systems-302186596.html
  - **Round 1's "fine-tuned on ~24M students' behavioral data" claim remains UNVERIFIED against a primary Squirrel AI technical source after two rounds of searching.** It continues to appear only in secondary/aggregated coverage (search-engine summary blocks referencing the LAM launch, an AI Summit Seoul writeup, an AI Journal piece on a Harvard/MIT symposium talk) — none of which were fetched as primary technical documents this round. No engineering blog, technical paper, or conference-talk transcript with architecture-level detail (parameter counts, training methodology, evaluation benchmarks) was located.
  - The arXiv paper "Foundation Models for Education: Promises and Prospects" (arxiv.org/html/2405.10959v1) surfaced again this round as a possible academic (non-Squirrel-authored) discussion of Squirrel AI's approach — not fetched this round; flagged as a lead.

- **TAL/好未来's AI-teacher stack, per Chinese-language coverage, centers on speech recognition + facial-expression recognition feeding a "monitoring/supervision system," not a fine-tuned behavioral model comparable to Squirrel AI's claims.** The "AI老师监课系统" (AI Teacher Classroom Supervision System) is described as using self-developed speech recognition and facial-expression recognition to structure and visualize classroom data in real time, scoring teacher instruction on dimensions like "affinity, clarity, fluency, interaction, key points." A separate "AI老师" line for students uses the same recognition technologies to push personalized learning tasks. The company's stated strategic goal is a "data flywheel" giving every teacher an AI teaching assistant and every child an AI learning companion. [source: 浙江在线 (zjol.com.cn) coverage of 学而思网校's AI老师 product launch at World Internet Conference, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: medium (Chinese trade press, not TAL's own engineering material), class: architecture pattern] https://edu.zjol.com.cn/jyjsb/zxx/201910/t20191021_11227275.shtml
  - No primary TAL engineering document (blog, paper) was located — this is trade-press description, consistent with round 1's finding that TAL's own technical material is not readily surfaced in general web search.

- **Yuanfudao/猿辅导's 2025 "小猿AI" educational-AI paradigm is explicitly described (in Chinese tech press) as a dual-model architecture — self-developed models plus general-purpose (presumably third-party/foundation) models — layered with a distinct "teaching logic" construction step, not a single fine-tuned monolith.** Reporting states the system is "built on data as infrastructure, with self-developed and general-purpose dual models as the core," covering correction/diagnosis, 1-on-1 tutoring, preview/review, and oral conversation. A named differentiator from "generic large model applications" is a "teaching thinking chain" ("教学思维链") built with senior teachers, following a stated pipeline of "teaching objective structure → element separation → technical reconstruction → dynamic debugging" to encode teacher expertise into the system rather than relying on the base model alone. The homework-grading product returns detailed error-type/step-level analysis within seconds and gives Socratic-style ("why," not the answer) 1:1 explanations. [source: 中国日报网 (chinadaily.com.cn tech channel) and 量子位/QbitAI coverage of 猿辅导's 小猿AI launch, April 2025, accessed 2026-08-22, confidence: medium (trade press, not Yuanfudao's own technical documentation), class: architecture pattern] https://tech.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202504/17/WS680071c2a310e29a7c4a9b3f.html ; https://www.qbitai.com/2025/04/274045.html
  - This is a materially more specific technical claim than what round 1 found for TAL/Yuanfudao (round 1 got "nothing usable"). Still trade-press level, not an engineering paper — treat the "dual-model" and "teaching thinking chain" framing as company-sourced marketing language relayed by press, not independently verified architecture.

- **Spheria.ai is confirmed to exist and to sell a "digital AI Twin for Teachers" product, with a stated mechanism of a personal knowledge base ("Virtual Brain") plus voice cloning — but exact pricing could not be retrieved this round (the pricing page 404'd on direct fetch).** Per search-snippet-level content from the company's own site: teachers upload "publications, papers, research or documents" to build a "Virtual Brain"; the AI answers "instantly" from that base; a newer feature adds two-way voice conversation with a cloned teacher voice ("clone your voice... in 30 seconds or less, no code needed") and image-relevant responses; the product is shareable via a single link and is marketed as used by teachers from Harvard Business School, University of Virginia, and University of Catalonia. [source: Spheria.ai own site (spheria.ai/ai-twin/digital-ai-twin-for-teachers-create-ai-clone and related pages), accessed 2026-08-22 via search snippet only — NOT independently fetched, confidence: low-medium, class: product description] https://spheria.ai/ai-twin/digital-ai-twin-for-teachers-create-ai-clone
  - This reads as a RAG-style architecture (knowledge base + retrieval to ground answers) combined with a separate voice-cloning feature for the conversational/audio layer — consistent with round 1's general finding that most "AI persona" products are prompt/RAG-grounded rather than deeply fine-tuned. However, this inference is not confirmed by any technical documentation — Spheria publishes no engineering blog or architecture page found this round, only marketing copy.
