# Ecosystem R2-1: Vietnam Regulatory Landscape + PlayHT Verification

## Findings

### 1. Vietnam personal data protection law — active transition, materially stricter incoming
- **Nghị định 13/2023/NĐ-CP** (the personal data protection decree Round 1 flagged) is being superseded. **Law No. 91/2025/QH15 on Personal Data Protection**, enacted 26 June 2025, took effect **1 January 2026**. [Asia IP](https://www.asiaiplaw.com/index.php/sector/patents/vietnam-releases-first-draft-of-new-law-on-personal-data-protection) | [LuatVietnam — Law 91/2025 overview](https://luatvietan.vn/nhung-diem-moi-cua-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-2025-so-voi-nghi-dinh-13-2023-nd-cp.html)
- The implementing decree, **Nghị định 356/2025/NĐ-CP**, also effective 1 January 2026, replaces Decree 13/2023 entirely and adds: mandatory retained proof of consent (default/pre-ticked consent is banned), stricter cross-border data transfer procedures, formal DPO qualification/training requirements, new reporting templates, and a coordinated inspection mechanism via the Cybersecurity Department (A05). [LuatVietnam — Decree 356/2025 vs 13/2023](https://luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/diem-moi-cua-nghi-dinh-356-2025-so-voi-nghi-dinh-13-2023-ve-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-568-106269-article.html)
- The Personal Data Protection Law explicitly names **children's data as a distinct sensitive category**, alongside health, biometric, location and credit data. [KPMG — draft PDP law overview](https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2024/09/an-overview-of-vietnam-draft-personal-data-protection-law.html)
- **Implication for AI Song Hành**: as of Jan 1 2026 the company is already operating under the new, stricter regime (Law 91/2025 + Decree 356/2025), not the older Decree 13/2023 that Round 1 found. Consent-capture, DPO staffing, and children's-data handling should be checked against Decree 356/2025 specifically.

### 2. Vietnam now has a standalone AI Law with provisions directly on-point for this product — verified against primary text
- **Law No. 134/2025/QH15 (Law on Artificial Intelligence)**, passed by the National Assembly 10 December 2025, effective **1 March 2026**, with an **18-month transition period for AI systems in education, healthcare, and finance** (i.e., compliance deadline ~1 September 2027 for an EdTech product), 12 months for other sectors. Risk-based framework modeled partly on the EU AI Act. [LuatVietnam English — full law text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/law-no-134-2025-qh15-dated-december-10-2025-of-the-national-assembly-on-artificial-intelligence-422299-doc1.html) | [Lexology — key takeaways](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=4fe57b61-d316-41d3-b68d-a10ce201735a)
- Directly checked the primary text via WebFetch of the LuatVietnam English translation. Provisions most relevant to "AI Song Hành":
  - **Article 7(2)(c)**: prohibits AI applications that exploit the vulnerabilities of specified groups — **children** are explicitly named — to cause harm. This is a direct legal constraint on a persistent AI-persona/companion product aimed at minors.
  - **Article 6(2)(b)**: education-sector AI must ensure "suitability for age characteristics and development of learners" and must not produce adverse classification/assessment impacts on learners — a direct obligation for an AI tutor persona.
  - **Article 11(4)**: mandates **"easily recognisable labels"** on audio content that simulates or replicates the **voice of a real person** — i.e., a Vietnam-specific statutory voice-cloning disclosure requirement. **Article 11(2)** additionally requires machine-readable marking of AI-generated audio/image/video content generally.
  - The law does **not** contain an explicit standalone "biometric data" regime for voice; voice-cloning is addressed only through the labeling/disclosure duty above, not through a consent-to-clone regime as such. Biometric-specific handling would fall under the Personal Data Protection Law/Decree 356/2025 instead.
- **This directly closes part of Round 1's Vietnam gap**: there is now codified, sector-specific Vietnamese law bearing on both "AI targeting children" and "voice cloning disclosure" — not just an absence of regulation.

### 3. MOET has an active, formal position on AI in K-12 — but it is curriculum-focused, not persona/companion-product-focused
- MOET issued **Official Dispatch (Công văn) 7652/BGDĐT-GDPT**, 21 November 2025, circulating **draft guidelines for a pilot AI-education curriculum** in general education to provincial education departments for comment. [Chính phủ policy portal](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/trien-khai-thi-diem-noi-dung-giao-duc-ai-trong-giao-duc-pho-thong-huong-dan-va-yeu-cau-119251219161637445.htm) | [VietnamPlus — MOET AI-in-training regulation](https://www.vietnamplus.vn/bo-gd-dt-ban-hanh-quy-dinh-ve-su-dung-ai-trong-dao-tao-nghien-cuu-khoa-hoc-post1121996.vnp)
- Pilot window: **December 2025 – May 2026**, 16 lessons/year across all grade levels, developed with the Vietnam Institute of Educational Sciences; explicit requirement that "AI education content and activities must be designed appropriately for the psychology, needs, and technology access capabilities of students at each level."
- This is MOET introducing AI **literacy curriculum for students to learn about AI** — it is not guidance on, or a restriction of, third-party commercial AI-tutor/AI-persona products used by students outside school. No evidence found of MOET issuing a position specifically on commercial AI companion/tutor products for K-12 (as distinct from AI-literacy curriculum).

[VOV2 — Bảo vệ trẻ em trước rủi ro AI](https://vov2.vov.vn/doi-song-xa-hoi/bao-ve-tre-em-truoc-cac-rui-ro-tu-tri-tue-nhan-tao-ai-51787.vov2) | [vMixGPT — Character.AI under-18 ban coverage](https://vmixgpt.com/character-ai-cam-nguoi-dung-duoi-18-tuoi-tro-chuyen-ai-mo-vi-lo-ngai-an-toan/)

## Leads
- **Most important**: Vietnam's new **AI Law (134/2025/QH15) Article 7(2)(c)** — the explicit statutory prohibition on AI applications that exploit children's vulnerabilities — plus **Article 11(4)**'s mandatory voice-clone disclosure labeling, are the two most decision-relevant, verified-against-primary-text findings. These should go directly into the risk assessment as concrete Vietnamese legal constraints (not just "global category risk"), with the added fact that education-sector AI systems get an 18-month transition window (to ~Sept 2027) before full compliance is required.
- Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP's ban on default/pre-ticked consent and its formal DPO-training requirement are worth a follow-up read of the full decree text (only summarized via secondary Vietnamese legal-commentary sites here, not fetched from a government portal) if the team wants exact obligations for children's-data consent flows.
- For the PlayHT customer-impact claims, a next attempt could try web.archive.org snapshots of Play.ht's status/communications pages, or a Google cache of the Trustpilot/G2 pages, since direct fetch was blocked by anti-bot protection rather than absence of content.
